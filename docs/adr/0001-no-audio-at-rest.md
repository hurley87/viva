# No Session audio at rest

Sessions are voice conversations, but we persist only the transcript — raw audio is never stored. Formative-only stakes make transcript disputes low-consequence, and audio storage would meaningfully raise the privacy surface inside a TCPS 2 QI carve-out at a data-sensitive institution (military college). We accept the cost: ASR errors (notably for accented speech, a known risk) cannot be verified against a recording, so a "the transcript misheard me" claim is resolved by Teacher judgment, not evidence. Revisit before any stakes-bearing deployment.
