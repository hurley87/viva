# Formative-only stakes for deployment #1

Sessions carry no course marks — unlimited informal attempts, feedback only. This was a real trade-off: stakes would drive adoption, but they drag in proctoring, identity assurance, appeal flows, and a research-ethics posture the pilot's TCPS 2 QI carve-out can't hold. Formative-only keeps the build small and the REB story boring. Reversing this mid-pilot is effectively a new product; any stakes-bearing deployment (v2 litigation track, graded coursework) reopens the accepted spoken prompt-injection risk and the no-audio-at-rest decision (ADR-0001).
