# Viva × Cosmos Grants

## The Pitch

As AI produces everyone's written work, the scarce thing is no longer the artifact — it's demonstrated ownership of the reasoning behind it. Detection asks "did AI write this?" and is losing. Viva makes the question irrelevant.

Viva is an AI oral examiner. A Defender defends work they claim as theirs — live, in voice — probed by an AI whose one structural constraint is that it never supplies the Defender's position. It can only question, challenge, and pressure. The evaluation standard is authored by a human expert, not the AI. Human judgment stays where it belongs on both sides of the exam: the expert defines what a competent defense must demonstrate, and the Defender demonstrates it.

**Three load-bearing differentiators:**

1. **Speech is an escape-hatch-free format.** No copy-paste, no passive consumption, no model supplying the position mid-sentence. You either command your own reasoning in real time or you don't.
2. **Defense of a concrete owned artifact.** Not abstract Socratic discussion — your paper, your brief, your work, under pressure.
3. **Autonomy by construction, not by instruction.** There is no path through the interaction where the AI hands the Defender an answer. The constraint is architectural (server-side instructions, output guardrails, independent audit), not a prompt asking nicely.

**The primitive generalizes.** Artifact / Standard / Session / Assessment; roles Author and Defender. Professor–student is one instantiation; partner–associate, hiring manager–candidate, and reviewer–engineer are others. Viva is the proof-of-understanding layer for an AI-saturated world.

**The 90-day deliverable:** a working voice prototype (Next.js / OpenAI Realtime over WebRTC) piloted in a real university course. The professor is an embedded design partner authoring evaluation standards; students defend their own papers in weeks 5–9 of term. The pilot runs as pedagogy — expert-authored standards, live sessions, the professor's examiner judgment as the evidence of whether a session functions as a true viva.

## The Program

**Cosmos Grants — AI for Human Autonomy track**, run by the [Cosmos Institute](https://www.cosmos-institute.org/), a 501(c)(3) non-profit funded by philanthropic donations. Cosmos describes itself as "the Academy for Philosopher-Builders" — technologists building AI for human flourishing — running fellowships, funding prototypes, and hosting seminars with institutions like Oxford, Google DeepMind, and Liberty Fund.

**Grant mechanics:**

- $1k–10k+ per grant; **Viva's award: $5,000**
- 90-day build project with a major deliverable expected at the end
- Rolling review, decisions in ~4 weeks; modeled on Tyler Cowen's Emergent Ventures — fast, minimal administrative overhead
- Round closed July 26, 2026; acceptance received July 27, 2026
- Program co-led by Alex Komoroske, Zoe Weinberg, and Darren Zhu (Kush handles grants ops)
- Beyond funding: community collaboration, potential follow-on funding, and possible feature on the Cosmos Substack
- Winners announced publicly after the full batch is confirmed (August 2026); kickoff call with other grantees around the same time

**The track's thesis:** writing let us externalize memory; the printing press relieved us of copying — technologies that extended our ability to act while preserving our capacity to form judgments. AI tempts us to offload deliberation itself. Autonomy is the faculty most at risk and most worth preserving, and the track funds builders whose work helps people avoid deferring too much to AI and instead practice judgment and self-formation.

(The Institute's second track — AI for Truth-Seeking — runs in partnership with FIRE with compute from Prime Intellect. Viva is in the autonomy track.)

**Past grant projects, i.e. the bar:**

- **Priori** — surfaces the silent value-choices behind an AI answer and makes them adjustable; founder became a Cosmos Fellow studying its effects on autonomy
- **MoSaIC** — AI conversation analysis + expert human evaluation of collaborative learning; became published research (ISLS CSCL, IEEE FIE) and part of an MIT Media Lab PhD
- **Thought Refractor** — renders arguments as multiple visual structures; became the startup Wondering
- **TLM-1** — a time-aware language model; paper accepted to ACL 2026
- **Prompt injection as role confusion** — won OpenAI's red-teaming contest; accepted to ICML
- **Campus** — project-based learning tool; founder went on to start Primitive

The pattern: Cosmos prototypes become fellowships, published papers, and companies. That's the frame for what Viva becomes after the 90 days.
